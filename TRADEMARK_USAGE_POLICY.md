# DDCP Trademark Usage Policy

DDCP is a trademark of DDCP Foundation Inc (United States application pending, classes 009 and 042). This policy states how the mark may be used by anyone who builds with the protocol, forks the reference implementation, writes about DDCP, or examines a currency against the DDCP conformance criteria. The code is licensed under Apache-2.0, which grants no trademark rights; this policy is the only grant.

Published by DDCP Foundation Inc. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## 1. What the mark covers

The mark is the word DDCP in any capitalization, alone or with a prefix or suffix, and any variant close enough to be mistaken for it. "DDCP Foundation" and "ddcp.dev" are names of the Foundation and its site and are used only by the Foundation. The Foundation has published no logo; none is licensed.

## 2. Permitted without asking

- **Referring to the protocol.** Writing "DDCP" or "the DDCP protocol" to name the protocol, discuss it, compare it or criticize it.
- **Stating provenance.** A currency built by forking the reference implementation may state, in words of its own: "Built as a fork of the DDCP reference implementation." A currency built without the reference code may state: "Based on the DDCP protocol." Either statement is provenance only: it says where the design came from, not that the currency meets any criterion. Either statement is accompanied by the issuer's own name as the issuer, and by the statement in section 4 when it appears in public material.
- **Package and crate names required by the reference.** The `ddcp-` prefix on Rust packages, which the reference implementation requires a fork to keep, and references to the DDCP specification, license and notice files. The npm scope `@ddcpfoundation` is the Foundation's; a fork publishes under its own scope.
- **Citing an evaluation.** Quoting a published DDCP conformance evaluation by linking to its entry in the registry and reproducing the per-criterion results as published, without summary, selection or badge.

## 3. Not permitted

- Using DDCP, or a variant that could be mistaken for it, in the name, symbol or ticker of any currency, product, service, company, domain or social account.
- Naming a currency, product or service so that it appears to be issued, operated, endorsed, recognized or recommended by the Foundation. A currency name or ticker beginning with "DD" followed by a currency or unit designation is treated as suggesting such an association and requires written permission under section 7; permissions granted are published in this repository.
- Describing any currency as "conforming", "DDCP-conforming", "DDCP-certified", "DDCP-compliant" or in equivalent terms, unless a published evaluation in the registry records every criterion as delivered, and then only by citing that evaluation. No badge, seal or mark of conformance exists.
- Describing a fork as "the DDCP reference implementation", or presenting any currency as a Foundation currency. The Foundation issues, operates and controls no currency.
- Attributing to a currency any commitment the Manifesto makes for the protocol. What a currency can do is a question about that currency, answered in its own specification.
- Registering, or applying to register, DDCP or a confusingly similar term as a trademark, company name or domain anywhere.

## 4. Required statement

Public material about a currency that references DDCP under section 2 carries, where the reference first appears, a statement to this effect: "DDCP is a trademark of DDCP Foundation Inc. The Foundation does not issue, operate, control or endorse this currency." Where the material also claims any of the currency's properties, it links to the currency's own specification and, if one exists, to its DDCP conformance evaluation.

## 5. Currency names

Currency names are the issuer's own and are not granted or approved by the Foundation. A name that contains neither DDCP nor a confusing variant, and is not of the form excluded in section 3, needs no permission. The reference implementation's placeholder `ddc` is not a currency name and is replaced in every fork; a public name beginning with "ddc" is treated as a confusing variant.

## 6. Foundation documents

The Manifesto, the conformance criteria, the evaluation methodology, this policy and the published evaluations are licensed under CC BY 4.0 and may be reproduced with attribution. Reproducing them does not permit any use of the mark that section 3 excludes.

## 7. Permission

Any use not covered by section 2 requires written permission from the Foundation, requested through the issues of this repository or contact@ddcpfoundation.org. The Foundation grants no exclusive rights to anyone, and every permission it grants is published.

## 8. Enforcement and amendment

The Foundation asks for the correction of a use that breaches this policy before taking any other step. Amendments follow the protocol change policy; a permitted use that a later version excludes gets a published transition period of at least ninety days.
