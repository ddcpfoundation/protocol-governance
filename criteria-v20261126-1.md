# Rationale: DDCP Evaluation Criteria, criteria-v20261126-1

Published by DDCP Foundation Inc under the protocol change policy, section 3, before the change takes effect. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## What changes

1. CRITERIA.md: fourteen criteria replace fifteen, renumbered, and the published label becomes "DDCP Evaluation Criteria".
2. EVALUATION_METHODOLOGY.md: the label becomes "DDCP Evaluation Methodology"; an evaluation of an implementation as code may record a fourth result; every criterion number it cites follows the new numbering.
3. PROTOCOL_CHANGE_POLICY.md: references to the criteria use the new label. Section 2 is not changed.

## Why

- **Basics and payments comes first.** A currency must work as money before its other criteria matter.
- **Privacy of parties and timing.** Privacy of sender and receiver and privacy of timing and frequency become one criterion. Both concern what the chain reveals about who transacts and when, and are examined on the same evidence.
- **Order.** The criteria examined on the mint and its program (control to authority allocation) now come before those that depend on intermediaries and reserves.
- **Lawful access only through judicial process.** The criterion now names the identified person and reads "only", so that identity reachable by administrative means does not meet it.
- **Accurate disclosure of security and capabilities.** The criterion now also covers the cryptographic and security assumptions a currency relies on, whether its code has had an independent security audit and which commit it covers, and what holders are exposed to if an assumption fails, including encrypted history already recorded on chain. Resistance to a quantum computer is named in "How the criteria are read".
- **Unconditionality.** The chain-specific mechanism moves out of the criterion into "How the criteria are read", as an example. The sentence on programs a holder chooses is reworded for clarity: "Programs a holder chooses to send value to are not affected" becomes "Conditions set by a program a holder chooses to use are not conditions of the currency". It covers the same programs, those a holder chooses, and requires nothing less of the currency itself. No item of the never-merge list is weakened.
- **A fourth result for code.** An implementation examined as code cannot deliver what depends on choices each currency makes, such as fee ceiling values, who holds each key, intermediaries and reserves. Recording those criteria as partly or not delivered described the method, not the code. "Not deliverable by code alone" is recorded only where every part the code does not deliver could be delivered by a currency's choices, never for a currency, and always with the choices that would defeat the criterion.

## Effect on published evaluations

Evaluations published under criteria-v20261008-2 keep their numbering and are not edited. Under the methodology, section 10, they are marked stale until a new evaluation replaces them.
